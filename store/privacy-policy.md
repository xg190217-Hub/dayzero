# DayZero Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 19, 2026*

DayZero is a private quit-habit tracker. Privacy is the foundation of this app — we designed it so that you never have to trust us with your data, because **we never receive it**.

## Data We Collect

**Nothing.** DayZero collects no personal data, no usage data, no analytics, and no diagnostics. The app has no account system, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

## Where Your Data Lives

Everything you enter — your habits, quit dates, check-ins, notes, reasons, and settings — is stored **only on your device**, in a local database. It never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

- Your data is included in your device backups (iCloud / iTunes) only if you enable device backups — Apple's own backup services handle that, under Apple's privacy policy.
- You can export your data as a JSON file at any time from Settings → Export my data.
- You can permanently delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete all data.

## Purchases

DayZero offers optional Premium subscriptions and a Lifetime unlock through Apple's In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (StoreKit); we do not see or receive your payment details. Apple may process transaction data under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Notifications

If you enable the daily check-in reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device. No notification content or interaction data is sent anywhere.

## Children

DayZero does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
