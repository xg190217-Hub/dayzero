# DayZero Support

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data private?**
Yes. DayZero has no account, no cloud and no analytics. Everything is stored only on your device.

**How do I restore my Premium purchase?**
Open Settings → DayZero Premium → Restore purchases. Make sure you use the same Apple ID you purchased with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → DayZero → Cancel.

**I slipped. Does my progress reset?**
No. When you tap "I slipped", you choose: restart the counter or keep going. All your check-ins and milestones remain in your stats.

**How do I export or delete my data?**
Settings → Export my data (saves a JSON file), or Settings → Delete all data.

**Can I add my own habit?**
Yes — choose "My own habit" when adding a habit and give it a name.

**Does DayZero replace professional treatment?**
No. DayZero is a motivation and tracking tool, not medical advice. For severe dependence, please consult a doctor.
